WELCOME TO QHOTELS MANAGEMENT

Formed in 1997, QHotels Management started with one Hotel and a philosophy of providing extraordinary service balanced with above average operating results. The original group of visionary entrepreneurs are now board members. QHotels Management has earned a national reputation as an innovator in owning, managing, marketing, and developing distinctive lodging entities. Led by distinguished industry veterans with more than one hundred and fifty years' experience, QHotels Management focuses on relentless, hands-on execution by its core executive team to continually improve asset value for hotel owners.
